About the Chapter

The SIAM Student Chapter at Purdue University is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting applied mathematics, computational science, and data science. Through weekly seminars, an annual student conference, and social events, we foster an inclusive community for undergraduates, graduate students, and postdocs across the Math, CS, Statistics, ECE, ME, and IE programs.
