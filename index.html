Who We Are

A community for applied & computational math at Purdue.

The SIAM Student Chapter at Purdue University is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting applied mathematics, computational science, and data science across our campus.

Open to all graduate and undergraduate students — SIAM Membership is FREE for students, details can be found here.
