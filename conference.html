About the Conference

The annual Purdue SIAM Student Conference brings together undergraduate and graduate students interested in applied mathematics, computational science, and data science. The conference provides a platform for students to present their research, learn from peers, and network with faculty and industry professionals.

Registration: Details coming soon.

Conference Format

Oral Presentations

Students present their research in 15–20 minute talks followed by a short Q&A.

Keynote Speakers

Invited talks from distinguished researchers in applied mathematics and computational science.

Networking

Coffee breaks, lunch sessions, and social hours to encourage conversation across departments.

Call for Abstracts

We invite submissions for oral presentations in all areas of applied mathematics, including:

  • Numerical analysis and scientific computing
  • Mathematical modeling and simulation
  • Data science and machine learning
  • Optimization and control theory
  • Computational biology and bioinformatics
  • Applied differential equations
  • Computational fluid dynamics
